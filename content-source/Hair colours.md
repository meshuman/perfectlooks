# Hair colours

12 hair colours. The level number tells the barber which shade to use from any brand. Henna is a plant colour, so it has no level. Hair dye can cause allergy, so every colour starts with an allergy question. For first-time dye, most boxes ask for a skin test 48 hours before.

## C01 Jet Black

Deep, solid black. Covers all grey.

**Description:** The deepest black. Covers all grey with a strong, even colour.

- **Pick this if:** you want all grey gone with a strong black.
- **Skip if:** you have light skin or lots of grey. Natural Black looks softer.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Wash with colour-safe shampoo.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks, for the roots
- **At the shop:** 45 min
- **Suits:** Medium and deep skin · Some or lots of grey
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **Does jet black cover all grey?** Yes. Level 1 covers grey fully.
- **Will jet black look natural?** On medium and deep skin, usually yes. On light skin or very grey hair it can look harsh, so Natural Black is softer.
- **How long does jet black last?** The colour stays, but roots show in 3–4 weeks.
- **Do I need a test before dyeing?** Yes. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions) advises testing the dye on a strand of hair at least 48 hours before.

**Barber steps**

1. Ask: itching from dye before? If yes, stop.
2. Colour: Level 1, black.
3. Cream on hairline and ears.
4. Apply on roots first, then the rest.
5. Wait time: as on the box.
6. Rinse well. Conditioner.

## C06 Golden Brown Highlights

Thin golden-brown streaks on dark hair.

**Description:** Thin golden-brown streaks through the top. Adds shine without colouring everything.

- **Pick this if:** you want a light, fun change and have some length on top.
- **Skip if:** your hair is very short.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Wash with colour-safe shampoo.
- **Next barber visit:** in 6–8 weeks
- **At the shop:** 60–90 min
- **Suits:** Light and medium skin · No grey or some grey
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What are highlights?** Thin streaks, lighter than your natural hair, placed through the top.
- **Do highlights need bleach?** Yes. The streaks are lightened first.
- **Do highlights work on short hair?** Not really. They need some length on top to show.
- **How long do highlights last?** They grow out over 6–8 weeks.

**Barber steps**

1. Ask: itching from dye before? If yes, stop.
2. Pick thin strands on top with a comb.
3. Lighten strands to Level 6–7.
4. Use foil or highlight cap.
5. Check every 10 min. Stop at golden brown.
6. Rinse. Toner if too orange. Conditioner.

## C07 Ash Grey / Silver

Light grey or silver all over. Bold look.

**Description:** Light grey or silver all over. A bold fashion look that needs bleaching first.

- **Pick this if:** you want the boldest colour change and have time for it.
- **Skip if:** your hair is damaged or your scalp is sensitive.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Purple shampoo 1–2 times to keep it silver.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks for toner, 3–4 weeks for roots
- **At the shop:** 2–3 hours, sometimes 2 visits
- **Suits:** All skin tones · Any amount of grey
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **Why does silver hair need bleach?** Dark hair has to be lightened almost to white before the silver toner can show.
- **Will silver hair turn yellow?** Over time it can. Purple shampoo keeps it silver.
- **Does bleach damage hair?** It can make hair dry and weak. Deep conditioner helps, and damaged hair should not be bleached.
- **How long does it take at the shop?** 2–3 hours, sometimes over 2 visits.

**Barber steps**

1. Ask: itching from dye before? If yes, stop.
2. Check scalp: no cuts or wounds.
3. Bleach whole head to Level 9–10.
4. Check every 10 min. Stop if burning.
5. Rinse. Then ash or silver toner.
6. Rinse. Deep conditioner.

Best done by a colour expert. May need 2 visits. Tell the customer to use purple shampoo.

## C08 Grey Blending

Softens grey without hiding it fully. Looks natural.

**Description:** Softens grey without hiding it fully, for a natural salt-and-pepper look.

- **Pick this if:** you want to look fresher, not dyed, with no hard root line.
- **Skip if:** you want zero grey.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Nothing extra.
- **Next barber visit:** in 4–6 weeks
- **At the shop:** 15–20 min
- **Suits:** All skin tones · Some or lots of grey
- **Also called:** Salt and pepper
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is grey blending?** A short, gentle colour that softens grey without hiding it fully.
- **Will people notice I coloured my hair?** Usually not. It looks like you naturally have less grey.
- **Will there be a hard root line?** No. It fades slowly over 4–6 weeks.
- **How long does it take?** 15–20 minutes.

**Barber steps**

1. Ask: itching from dye before? If yes, stop.
2. Colour: Level 3–4, semi-permanent.
3. Apply on grey areas.
4. Short time: 5–10 min only.
5. Goal: less grey, not zero grey.
6. Rinse well.

## C12 Henna (Mehndi)

Natural plant colour. Turns grey hair red-orange.

**Description:** A natural plant colour. Turns grey hair red-orange and gives dark hair a red shine.

- **Pick this if:** you want a natural colour with no chemicals.
- **Skip if:** you may want a chemical colour later. It is hard to change after henna.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Nothing extra.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks, for the roots
- **At the shop:** 1–2 hours
- **Suits:** All skin tones · Some or lots of grey
- **Also called:** Mehndi
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **Is henna better than hair dye?** Pure henna is plant-based, but on grey it only gives red-orange, and it's hard to change later.
- **Is "black henna" safe?** No. Its paste contains high levels of PPD, which can cause skin reactions, according to the [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions). Use pure henna only.
- **How long does henna stay on?** 1–2 hours.
- **Can I dye my hair after henna?** The result can be patchy or an unexpected colour. Tell your barber you've used henna.

**Barber steps**

1. Use pure henna only. Never "black henna".
2. Ask: itching from henna before? If yes, stop.
3. Apply paste on roots first, then the rest.
4. Cover hair with a cap.
5. Wait 1–2 hours.
6. Rinse with water only. No shampoo for 1 day.
