## M01 Short Trimmed

Neat, short moustache that stops at the lip.

**Description:** A simple, neat moustache trimmed short above the lip.

- **Pick this if:** you want a moustache that suits everyone and needs almost no care.
- **Skip if:** you want a bold statement.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Trim once at 3 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** with your haircut, in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** All face shapes · Any growth
- **Also called:** Simple moustache
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What length is a short trimmed moustache?** About 3 mm.
- **Should my moustache touch my lip?** No. Keep the bottom edge just above the lip.
- **How often should I trim it?** Once a week.
- **Does it go with a beard?** Yes, with any beard that doesn't have its own moustache.

**Barber steps**

1. Comb moustache down.
2. Whole moustache: trimmer 3 mm.
3. Bottom edge: just above the lip.
4. Ends: stop at mouth corners.
5. Remove stray hair.

## M02 Chevron

Thick and wide. Covers the top edge of the lip.

**Description:** A thick, wide moustache that covers the top edge of the lip. Classic and strong.

- **Pick this if:** you grow a thick moustache and want a manly, classic look.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy.
- **Daily:** 1 min: comb down.
- **Weekly:** Trim the lip edge once.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square and round face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a chevron moustache?** A thick, wide moustache shaped like a roof, covering the top edge of the lip.
- **How long does it take to grow?** About 4–6 weeks of growth.
- **Does a chevron need wax?** No. Just comb it down.
- **Which face shapes suit a chevron?** Oval, square and round faces.

**Barber steps**

1. Comb moustache down.
2. Keep it thick. Do not thin out.
3. Bottom edge: covers top edge of lip.
4. Ends: to mouth corners.
5. Shape: wide, like a roof.
6. Trim both sides even.

## M03 Pencil

Very thin line just above the lip.

**Description:** A very thin line just above the lip. A sharp, old film-star look.

- **Pick this if:** you want a neat, retro detail.
- **Skip if:** you have a wide face, or can't shave often.
- **Daily:** 3 min: shave above the line, every day or every second day.
- **Weekly:** Trim the line at 1 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, long and heart face · Any growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How thin is a pencil moustache?** About 3 mm tall.
- **Is a pencil moustache hard to keep?** Yes. It needs shaving above the line every day or two.
- **Does it suit a wide face?** Not really. It can look too thin on a wide face.
- **Can I have one with a patchy beard?** Yes. It needs very little growth.

**Barber steps**

1. Trimmer: 1 mm.
2. Line: thin, about 3 mm tall.
3. Line sits just above the lip.
4. Small gap in the middle, under the nose.
5. Shave above the line with razor.

## M04 Handlebar

Long ends curled up. Bold, classic look.

**Description:** A full moustache with long ends curled up. Bold and traditional.

- **Pick this if:** you want to stand out at weddings and festivals.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy or medium, or you don't want daily styling.
- **Daily:** 5 min: wax and twist the ends.
- **Weekly:** Trim the middle above the lip.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, long and heart face · Full growth only
- **Also called:** Curled moustache
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How long does it take to grow a handlebar?** Usually 3–6 months for the ends to curl.
- **What wax should I use?** A firm moustache wax that holds the curl all day.
- **Can I eat easily with a handlebar?** Yes. Keep the middle trimmed above the lip.
- **Is a handlebar good for weddings?** Yes. It's a popular look for festivals and weddings.

**Barber steps**

1. Do not cut the ends.
2. Middle: trim above lip line.
3. Ends: keep long.
4. Wax the ends.
5. Twist ends up into curls.
6. Shave around the moustache.

## M05 Horseshoe

Moustache runs down both sides of the mouth to the jaw.

**Description:** The moustache runs down both sides of the mouth to the jaw, like an upside-down U.

- **Pick this if:** you want a tough, bold look.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face or patchy growth.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Shave the chin 2–3 times.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round and square face · Full growth only
- **Also called:** Biker moustache
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a horseshoe moustache?** A moustache that runs down both sides of the mouth to the jaw, like an upside-down U.
- **Do I need full growth?** Yes. The side lines need thick growth all the way to the jaw.
- **Does a horseshoe suit a long face?** No. The down lines make a long face look longer.
- **How do I keep the lines neat?** Shave the chin 2–3 times a week and keep each line about 1 finger wide.

**Barber steps**

1. Keep moustache full on top.
2. Down lines: both sides of mouth to the jaw.
3. Down line width: 1 finger.
4. All hair: trimmer 5 mm.
5. Shave chin and cheeks clean.
6. Sharp edges with razor.

## M06 English

Thin and long, with pointed ends going out to the sides.

**Description:** A thin moustache with long ends brushed straight out to the sides.

- **Pick this if:** you want a neat, classic style with character.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy.
- **Daily:** 3 min: wax the ends into points.
- **Weekly:** Trim the middle above the lip.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval and long face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is an English moustache?** A thin moustache with long ends brushed straight out to the sides.
- **How is it different from a handlebar?** Handlebar ends curl up. English ends point straight out.
- **Do I need wax?** Yes. A little wax keeps the ends pointed.
- **How long does it take to grow?** About 2–3 months for the ends to reach past the mouth.

**Barber steps**

1. Middle: short, above the lip.
2. Ends: keep long. Do not cut.
3. Wax the ends.
4. Pull ends straight out, pointed.
5. Shave around the moustache.

## M07 Walrus

Big and thick. Hangs over the top lip.

**Description:** A big, thick moustache that hangs over the top lip.

- **Pick this if:** you grow a very thick moustache and want a relaxed, mature look.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy, or you don't like hair over your lip.
- **Daily:** 1 min: comb down.
- **Weekly:** Trim the bottom edge once.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval and square face · Full growth only
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a walrus moustache?** A big, thick moustache that hangs over the top lip.
- **Does it get in the way when eating?** It can. Trim the bottom edge to show as much lip as you like.
- **How long does it take to grow?** About 3–4 months.
- **Which face shapes suit a walrus?** Oval and square faces.

**Barber steps**

1. Comb moustache down.
2. Keep it long and thick.
3. Ask: how much lip to show?
4. Bottom edge: trim straight.
5. Remove stray hair only.

## M08 Lampshade

Short and wide at the bottom, narrow at the top.

**Description:** A short moustache, narrow at the top and wide at the bottom, above the lip.

- **Pick this if:** you want a neat, old-school shape with little care.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Trim once at 3 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round and square face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a lampshade moustache?** A short moustache that is narrow at the top and wide at the bottom, like a lamp shade.
- **What length is it?** About 3 mm.
- **Is it hard to keep?** No. A weekly trim keeps the shape.
- **Does it go with a beard?** Yes, with any beard that doesn't have its own moustache.

**Barber steps**

1. Whole moustache: trimmer 3 mm.
2. Bottom edge: straight, above the lip.
3. Sides: straight, angled out.
4. Top narrow, bottom wide.
5. Shave around the moustache.

## M09 Natural Full

Medium-thick moustache in its natural shape, trimmed at the lip.

**Description:** The everyday moustache: medium thickness, natural shape, trimmed off the lip.

- **Pick this if:** you want a mature, tidy moustache that suits almost everyone.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy.
- **Daily:** 1 min: comb.
- **Weekly:** Trim the lip edge once.
- **Next barber visit:** with your haircut, in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** All face shapes · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a natural full moustache?** The everyday moustache: medium thickness, natural shape, trimmed so it doesn't touch the lip.
- **What trimmer length should I use?** About 6 mm.
- **How often should I trim it?** Trim the lip edge once a week.
- **Does it suit older men?** Yes. It suits every age.

**Barber steps**

1. Comb moustache down.
2. Whole moustache: trimmer 6 mm.
3. Bottom edge: on the lip line.
4. Keep the natural shape.
5. Remove stray hair.

## M10 Petite Handlebar

Small handlebar with short ends turned up.

**Description:** A small handlebar with short ends turned up a little.

- **Pick this if:** you like the handlebar look but your moustache isn't very long.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy.
- **Daily:** 2 min: a little wax on the ends.
- **Weekly:** Trim the middle above the lip.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square, long and heart face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How is a petite handlebar different from a handlebar?** The ends are shorter and only turn up a little.
- **How long does it take to grow?** About 6–8 weeks for the ends to reach past the mouth.
- **Do I need wax?** Yes, just a little on the ends.
- **Is it a good first handlebar?** Yes. It's easier to grow and style.

**Barber steps**

1. Middle: trim above lip line.
2. Ends: keep 1 finger past mouth corners.
3. Do not cut the ends short.
4. Wax the ends.
5. Turn the ends up a little.
6. Shave around the moustache.

## M11 Hungarian

Big, bushy moustache with the ends pulled out to the sides.

**Description:** A big, bushy moustache with the hair pulled out to both sides.

- **Pick this if:** you have very thick growth and want a bold, old-fashioned look.
- **Skip if:** you have a round face, or patchy or medium growth.
- **Daily:** 3 min: comb out to the sides, a little wax.
- **Weekly:** Trim the lip edge.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square and long face · Full growth only
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a Hungarian moustache?** A big, bushy moustache with the hair pulled out to both sides.
- **How long does it take to grow?** Usually 4–6 months of full growth.
- **Do I need wax?** A little wax on the ends holds the shape.
- **Does it suit a round face?** No. It makes the face look wider.

**Barber steps**

1. Keep it long and thick.
2. Comb hair from the middle out to the sides.
3. Bottom edge: trim off the lip.
4. Ends: keep long, pointing out.
5. Light wax on the ends.

## M12 Dali

Thin moustache with long ends pointing sharply up.

**Description:** A thin moustache with long, stiff ends pointing straight up. Artistic and eye-catching.

- **Pick this if:** you want the most unusual moustache in the shop.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy or medium, or you work in an office.
- **Daily:** 5 min: strong wax, twist the ends up.
- **Weekly:** Trim the middle above the lip.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, long and heart face · Full growth only
- **Also called:** Pointed-up moustache
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **Why is it called a Dali moustache?** It's named after the painter Salvador Dalí, who wore one.
- **How long does it take to grow?** Usually 6 months or more for the long ends.
- **What holds the ends up?** Strong moustache wax, twisted into points.
- **Is it practical for every day?** It needs about 5 minutes of styling daily, so it suits people who enjoy styling.

**Barber steps**

1. Middle: thin and short, above the lip.
2. Ends: keep very long. Do not cut.
3. Strong wax on the ends.
4. Twist the ends into thin points.
5. Push the ends straight up.
6. Shave around the moustache.
