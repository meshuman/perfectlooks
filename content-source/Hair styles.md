## H01 Buzz Cut

Very short, same length all over. Clean and easy.

**Description:** One short length all over, cut with the machine. Clean, cool and no fuss.

- **Pick this if:** you want zero styling and a cool, clean head.
- **Skip if:** you have a round or long face, or don't want your head shape to show.
- **Daily:** Nothing. Just wash.
- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval and square face · All hair types · Thin hair OK
- **Also called:** Machine cut, Army cut
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What machine number is a buzz cut?** Usually #2 (6 mm). Ask for #1 if you want it shorter, or #3 for a softer look.
- **Is a buzz cut good for hot weather?** Yes. With so little hair, your head stays cooler and dries in minutes.
- **Will a buzz cut show scars or bumps on my head?** At #1 or #2 they can show. Choose #3 or #4 for more cover.
- **How fast does a buzz cut grow out?** Hair grows about 1.25 cm a month, so a #2 buzz looks like a short crew cut after about a month.

**Barber steps**

1. Whole head: machine #2.
2. Cut against hair growth.
3. Neck line: clean with trimmer.
4. Clean around ears.

Customer may ask for #1 or #3 instead.

## H02 Crew Cut

Short sides, a little longer on top. Neat and simple.

**Description:** Short sides with a little more length on top. Neat at work, college or a family function.

- **Pick this if:** you want a safe, tidy cut that looks fine everywhere.
- **Skip if:** you have a very long face.
- **Daily:** 1 min: push the top forward or up with your hand.
- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square and round face · All hair types · Thin hair OK
- **Also called:** Short cut
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is the difference between a crew cut and a buzz cut?** A buzz cut is one length all over. A crew cut keeps the top a little longer than the sides.
- **What number is a crew cut?** Usually machine #2 on the sides and about 1 finger (2 cm) on top, cut with scissors.
- **Is a crew cut good for office?** Yes. It's one of the safest cuts for any office, school or family event.
- **Can I get a crew cut with curly hair?** Yes. The short length keeps curls neat and easy.

**Barber steps**

1. Sides and back: machine #2.
2. Top: 1 finger long. Scissors.
3. Front of top: a little longer.
4. Blend sides into top.
5. Clean neck and ears.

## H03 Ivy League

A longer crew cut. Top can be combed to the side.

**Description:** A longer crew cut with enough top to comb into a side part. Smart but still easy.

- **Pick this if:** you want an office-ready look that is still short.
- **Skip if:** you have a round face.
- **Daily:** 2 min: comb to the side with a little cream or wax.
- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square, long and heart face · Straight or wavy hair · Thin hair OK
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is the difference between a crew cut and an Ivy League?** An Ivy League keeps the top longer, about 2 fingers, so it can be combed into a side part.
- **Is the Ivy League good for thin hair?** Yes. The short sides make the top look fuller.
- **Can I wear an Ivy League without a part?** Yes. Push it forward or up with your fingers for a relaxed look.
- **How do I keep the side part neat?** Comb it while your hair is still a little damp, then add a little cream.

**Barber steps**

1. Sides and back: machine #2, blend to #3.
2. Top: 2 fingers long. Scissors.
3. Front: a little longer than back.
4. Ask: part on left or right?
5. Comb top to that side.
6. Clean neck and ears.

## H04 Caesar Cut

Short all over with a short straight fringe in front.

**Description:** Short all over, with a short straight fringe combed forward.

- **Pick this if:** you want to cover a high forehead or thin hair at the front.
- **Skip if:** you have a round face.
- **Daily:** 1 min: comb forward. No product needed.
- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square, long and heart face · All hair types · Thin hair OK
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **Does a Caesar cut hide a receding hairline?** Yes. The short fringe sits over the front and covers a high or thin hairline.
- **How is a Caesar cut different from a French crop?** A Caesar has a straight, flat fringe. A French crop has a rougher top.
- **Is a Caesar cut good for curly hair?** Yes. Short curls lie forward easily and need no styling.
- **Do I need product for a Caesar cut?** No. Just comb it forward. A little wax is optional.

**Barber steps**

1. Sides and back: machine #2.
2. Top: 1 finger long. Scissors.
3. Comb all top hair forward.
4. Fringe: cut straight across, short.
5. Clean neck and ears.

## H06 Taper Cut

Classic short cut. Gets shorter slowly near the ears and neck.

**Description:** The classic barber cut. Hair gets shorter slowly toward the ears and neck, with no skin showing.

- **Pick this if:** you want the safest choice for any face, age or job.
- **Skip if:** you want a bold, fashion look.
- **Daily:** 2 min: comb with a little cream.
- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** All face shapes · All hair types · Thin hair OK
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is the difference between a taper and a fade?** A taper gets shorter but stops before skin. A fade goes down to bare skin.
- **Is a taper cut good for office?** Yes. It's the most office-safe cut in the catalogue.
- **How long does a taper last?** It still looks neat after 3–4 weeks, because it grows out gently.
- **Does a taper suit older men?** Yes. It suits every age, face shape and hair type.

**Barber steps**

1. Sides and back: machine #3.
2. Near ears and neck: machine #1.
3. Blend slowly. No skin showing.
4. Top: 2 fingers long. Scissors.
5. Neck line: natural, not square.

- **Pick this if:** you want a clean, modern look that is still fine for school or office.

- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks, to keep the fade clean
- **Suits:** All face shapes · All hair types · Thin hair OK
- **Main photo:** Side

**FAQ**

- **How is a low fade different from a mid fade?** A low fade starts just above the ear. A mid fade starts higher, at the temple.
- **Is a low fade OK for school or office?** Usually yes. It's the most subtle fade.
- **What numbers are used for a low fade?** Usually #0 at the bottom, blended up through #1 to #2.

1. Fade line: 1 finger above the ear.
2. Below the line: machine #0.
3. Blend up: #1, then #2.
4. Top: 2 fingers long. Scissors.
5. Blend top into sides.
6. Clean neck and ears.

## H09 Skin Fade

Sides go to bare skin, high up. Very sharp look.

**Description:** Sides shaved to bare skin, fading up high. The sharpest, cleanest sides in the catalogue.

- **Pick this if:** you want the boldest, cleanest sides and don't mind frequent cuts.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face, or can't visit every 2 weeks.
- **Daily:** 2 min: a little dry wax (clay) on the top.
- **Weekly:** Nothing.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, square and round face · All hair types · Thin hair OK
- **Also called:** Bald fade, Zero fade, High fade
- **Main photo:** Side

**FAQ**

- **Is a skin fade the same as a bald fade?** Yes. Both take the sides down to bare skin with a razor or shaver.
- **How long does a skin fade stay sharp?** About 1–2 weeks. Book a touch-up every 2 weeks.
- **Will a skin fade irritate my skin?** It shouldn't. Tell the barber if your skin is sensitive, so they shave gently. On tightly curled hair, ask for a foil shaver instead of a razor, to avoid ingrown hairs.
- **Is a skin fade OK for office?** It depends on the office. A low fade or taper fade is the safer choice.

**Barber steps**

1. Fade line: high, at the top corner of the head.
2. Bottom: razor or shaver to skin.
3. Blend up: #0, #1, #2.
4. Smooth blend. No hard lines.
5. Top: 2 fingers long. Scissors.
6. Clean neck and ears.
