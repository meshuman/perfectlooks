# Learn

13 short posts for customers to read while they wait for their turn. Each one takes 1–2 minutes, answers one question people search for, and links to the styles it mentions, so reading leads straight to a decision. Every post is also a public web page that follows the rules in the Search and AI visibility tab.

## How Learn works in the app

- **Where:** a Learn button in the bottom bar, and a "While you wait" card on Home.
- **Feed:** one card per post, with a photo, the title and "1 min read".
- **Topics:** Face shape · Haircuts · Beard · Colour · Care · Talk to your barber.
- **Post screen:** the Short answer comes first. Below the post, "Styles in this post" shows each style with a heart and Add to My Look.
- **End of post:** Next post, and "Was this helpful? Yes / No".
- **First three posts in the feed:** P01 face shape, P06 clipper numbers, P07 how often to get a haircut. These help the most right before a cut.

## P01 What is my face shape? A 30-second guide for men

- **URL:** /learn/face-shape-guide · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Face shape
- **Meta description:** Find your face shape in 30 seconds with a mirror: oval, round, square, long or heart. See which haircuts and beards suit each one.

**Short answer:** Look straight into a mirror with your hair pulled back. Compare the width of your forehead, cheekbones and jaw, then compare length with width. Most men are oval, round, square, long or heart-shaped. Your shape decides where a haircut should add height, width or softness.

### How do I check my face shape?

1. Push your hair off your forehead.
2. Find the widest part: forehead, cheekbones or jaw.
3. Compare the length of your face with its width.
4. Match what you see to the table.

| Face shape | How to tell | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oval | A bit longer than wide, soft jaw | Almost any style |
| Round | Length and width about the same, soft jaw | Height on top: Quiff, Faux Hawk, Brush Up |
| Square | Strong, wide jaw | Short, neat cuts: Crew Cut, Classic Side Part |
| Long | Much longer than wide | A fringe or fuller sides: French Crop, Caesar Cut, Two-Block |
| Heart | Wide forehead, narrow chin | A fringe, and more beard at the chin: Side Fringe, Full Beard |

**From the chair:** Not sure? Pick the two closest shapes. A style that suits both is your safest choice.

**FAQ**

- **Can my face shape change?** The bone shape doesn't, but weight changes and a beard change how your face looks.
- **Can a beard change how my face looks?** Yes. More length at the chin makes a round face look longer. Fuller sides make a long face look wider.

**Related:** H14 Quiff · H05 French Crop · H02 Crew Cut · B05 Full Beard

## P02 Fade vs taper: what is the difference?

- **URL:** /learn/fade-vs-taper · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Haircuts
- **Meta description:** A fade goes down to skin; a taper gets shorter but keeps some hair. See which suits your job, your face and how often you can visit.

**Short answer:** A fade takes the sides down to bare skin, then blends up into longer hair. A taper also gets shorter toward the ears and neck, but stops before skin. Fades look sharper and need a cut every 2–3 weeks. Tapers look softer and last 3–4 weeks.

### Which one should I choose?

|  | Taper | Fade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shortest point | Short hair, no skin | Bare skin |
| Look | Soft, classic | Sharp, modern |
| Strict office or school | Always fine | Usually fine as a low fade |
| Next barber visit | In 3–4 weeks | In 2–3 weeks |

### Is there something in between?

Yes, the Taper Fade. Only the sideburns and the back of the neck go down to skin, so it looks clean but quiet.

**From the chair:** If your workplace is strict, start with a taper. You can always go shorter next time, but you can't add hair back.

**FAQ**

- **Does a fade grow out badly?** The sharp lines blur after 2–3 weeks, but it grows into a taper shape, so it still looks tidy.
- **Can I get a fade with curly hair?** Yes. The Curly Top Fade keeps the curls on top and fades the sides.

**Related:** H06 Taper Cut · H23 Taper Fade · H07 Low Fade · H09 Skin Fade

## P03 Low, mid or high fade: which one should you get?

- **URL:** /learn/which-fade · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Haircuts
- **Meta description:** Low, mid and high fades differ in where the skin part starts. Learn which height suits your face, your job and how often you can visit.

**Short answer:** The difference is where the fade starts. A low fade starts just above the ear, a mid fade at the temple, and a high fade near the top corner of the head. The higher the fade, the bolder the look and the sooner it needs a touch-up: about every 2 weeks for a high fade.

### How do I pick the height?

| Fade | Starts at | Best for | Next barber visit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low Fade (H07) | 1 finger above the ear | A first fade, office, school | In 2–3 weeks |
| Mid Fade (H08) | The temple | A sharper, modern look | In 2–3 weeks |
| Skin Fade, high (H09) | Top corner of the head | The boldest, cleanest sides | In 2 weeks |

### Which fade suits a long face?

A low fade or a taper. Very short sides placed high make a long face look longer.

**From the chair:** Show the barber the photo, then agree on the shortest number at the bottom, such as machine #0 or #1. A number avoids surprises.

**FAQ**

- **Which fade suits a round face?** A mid or high fade, with some height on top.
- **Do fades work with thin hair?** Yes. Short sides make the top look fuller.

**Related:** H07 Low Fade · H08 Mid Fade · H09 Skin Fade · H24 Drop Fade

## P04 Best haircuts for a round face (men)

- **URL:** /learn/haircuts-for-round-face · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Haircuts
- **Meta description:** For a round face, choose short sides with height on top. See the haircuts and beards that add length and shape, and the ones to skip.

**Short answer:** A round face looks best with short sides and some height on top, because that makes the face look longer. Good choices are the Quiff, Faux Hawk, Brush Up and Mid Fade. Skip flat, rounded cuts like the Mushroom Cut. A beard that is longer at the chin than at the sides also helps.

### Which haircuts work?

- **Quiff (H14):** height at the front
- **Faux Hawk (H17):** height in the middle
- **Brush Up (H38):** easy height for every day
- **Mid Fade (H08):** clean, short sides

### Which beards work?

- **Goatee (B06) or Van Dyke (B07):** length at the chin
- **Short Boxed Beard (B04):** square corners add shape

### What should I avoid?

The Mushroom Cut, Middle Part and Two-Block add width at the sides. A Buzz Cut shows the round shape clearly.

**From the chair:** The bigger the difference between short sides and a taller top, the longer your face looks.

**FAQ**

- **Is a beard good for a round face?** Yes, if it is longer at the chin than at the sides.
- **Can I have longer hair with a round face?** Medium length can work if it has height on top. Long, flat hair adds width.

**Related:** H14 Quiff · H17 Faux Hawk · H38 Brush Up · B06 Goatee

## P05 Haircuts for thin hair or a receding hairline

- **URL:** /learn/haircuts-for-thin-hair · **Read:** 2 min · **Topic:** Haircuts
- **Meta description:** Shorter cuts make thin hair look fuller. See the best haircuts for thin hair and a receding hairline, and when to see a doctor.

**Short answer:** Shorter is better. Short sides make the top look thicker, and a short fringe pushed forward covers a thin front. The French Crop, Crop Fade, Caesar Cut and Buzz Cut all work well. Avoid long, slicked-back styles like the Slick Back, which let the scalp show through.

### Why does short hair look thicker?

Long hair separates and lies flat, so the scalp shows between strands. Short hair stands up and looks denser.

### Which cuts work best?

- **French Crop (H05) and Crop Fade (H31):** the fringe covers the front
- **Caesar Cut (H04):** a short, straight fringe
- **Buzz Cut (H01) or Head Shave (H30):** no contrast between thin and thick areas

### Which product should I use?

Dry wax (clay). Shiny wax and heavy gel make hair clump together, which shows more scalp.

### When should I see a doctor?

Shedding 50–100 hairs a day is normal, and in men about 95% of hair loss is male pattern baldness, according to experts quoted by [USA Today](https://www.aol.com/fast-does-hair-actually-grow-090116698.html). See a doctor if hair falls out suddenly, in patches, or with an itchy or sore scalp.

**From the chair:** Tell your barber where it is thin. They can leave a little more length there and blend it in.

**FAQ**

- **Does cutting hair make it grow thicker?** No. Trimming keeps the ends healthy but doesn't change how hair grows, a dermatologist told USA Today.
- **Is a shaved head a good idea for thinning hair?** It's the cleanest option when thinning is spread across the top.

**Related:** H05 French Crop · H31 Crop Fade · H04 Caesar Cut · H30 Head Shave

## P06 Clipper numbers explained: how to ask your barber

- **URL:** /learn/clipper-numbers · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Talk to your barber
- **Meta description:** Machine #1 leaves 3 mm, #2 leaves 6 mm, #3 leaves 10 mm. Use clipper numbers to get exactly the haircut you want.

**Short answer:** Clipper guard numbers tell the barber how much hair to leave. Machine #0 leaves about 0.5–1 mm, #1 leaves 3 mm, #2 leaves 6 mm, #3 leaves 10 mm and #4 leaves 13 mm. Saying "sides #2, top 2 fingers" is much clearer than "short on the sides".

### What does each number leave?

| Machine | Hair left | Looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #0 | 0.5–1 mm | Skin shows through |
| #1 | 3 mm | Scalp visible |
| #2 | 6 mm | Very short and even |
| #3 | 10 mm | Short |
| #4 | 13 mm | Short but soft |
| #8 | 25 mm | Short to medium |

These are the most common guard sizes. Brands can differ by about a millimetre.

### What does "fingers" mean?

Barbers measure the top by finger width. One finger is about 2 cm of hair, so "2 fingers" means about 4 cm.

### What should I say?

Give three things: the sides, the top and the finish. For example: "Sides #1 faded to #3, top 2 fingers, clean neck line."

**From the chair:** Not sure? Ask for one number longer than you think. The barber can always go shorter.

**FAQ**

- **Is #1 shorter than #2?** Yes. A smaller number means shorter hair.
- **What is a half number like #1.5?** A size in between, about 4.5 mm, used to blend fades.

**Related:** H01 Buzz Cut · H02 Crew Cut · H07 Low Fade

## P07 How often should men get a haircut?

- **URL:** /learn/how-often-haircut · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Care
- **Meta description:** Most men need a haircut every 2–8 weeks. Hair grows about 1.25 cm a month, so faded cuts need a visit sooner. See the timing for your style.

**Short answer:** Every 2 to 8 weeks, depending on the style. Hair grows about half an inch (1.25 cm) a month, according to the American Academy of Dermatology Association. Fades and very short cuts lose their shape in 2–3 weeks, classic short cuts last 3–4 weeks, and medium or long hair needs a trim every 6–8 weeks.

### How often for my style?

| Style type | Next barber visit | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skin fade, line designs | In 2 weeks | H09, H25 |
| Fades, buzz cuts | In 2–3 weeks | H07, H21 |
| Classic short cuts | In 3–4 weeks | H02, H06, H10 |
| Medium styles | In 4–6 weeks | H18, H19 |
| Long hair | In 6–8 weeks | H20, H29, H40 |

### Does cutting make hair grow faster?

No. Trimming removes damaged ends but doesn't change how fast hair grows, a dermatologist told [USA Today](https://www.aol.com/fast-does-hair-actually-grow-090116698.html).

### When should I book before a wedding or festival?

One or two days before the wedding, Dashain or Tihar. A fresh fade can look too sharp on the same day. A day later it looks natural.

**From the chair:** Take a photo of your Barber Card before you leave. Next time, show it and get the same cut again.

**FAQ**

- **How much does hair grow in a month?** About 1.25 cm (half an inch) on average.
- **Can I wait longer between cuts?** Yes, but fades and shaved lines blur after about 3 weeks.

**Related:** H09 Skin Fade · H07 Low Fade · H06 Taper Cut · H20 Medium Flow

## P08 How to trim your beard neck line at home

- **URL:** /learn/beard-neck-line · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Beard
- **Meta description:** Trim your beard neck line 2 fingers above your Adam's apple. A simple 4-step guide to keep any beard neat between barber visits.

**Short answer:** Put two fingers above your Adam's apple: that is where your neck line goes. Trim everything below that line, curving back toward the ears. A clean neck line is the single biggest thing that makes any beard look tidy.

### How do I trim it, step by step?

1. Place 2 fingers above your Adam's apple.
2. Picture a curved line from there to just below each earlobe.
3. Trim everything below the line with a trimmer, no guard.
4. Shave that area for a clean finish.

### What mistakes should I avoid?

- **Cutting too high:** the beard starts to look like a chin strap and the jaw looks weak.
- **Cutting straight across:** follow a gentle curve up to the ears.

**From the chair:** Clean the neck line at home every 3–4 days. Let the barber reshape the beard every 2–3 weeks.

**FAQ**

- **Where should my beard end on my neck?** About 2 fingers above the Adam's apple.
- **Do I need a neck line with stubble?** Yes. It keeps even short stubble looking neat.

**Related:** B02 Light Stubble · B03 Heavy Stubble · B04 Short Boxed Beard · B05 Full Beard

## P09 Best beard styles for a patchy beard

- **URL:** /learn/patchy-beard-styles · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Beard
- **Meta description:** A patchy beard still has great options. Light stubble, goatee, Van Dyke, circle beard and anchor beard all work with gaps on the cheeks.

**Short answer:** Keep hair where it grows well and shave where it is thin. Light Stubble hides gaps because everything is short. The Goatee, Van Dyke, Circle Beard and Anchor Beard use only the chin and lip, so patchy cheeks don't matter.

### Which styles work?

| Style | Uses | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light Stubble (B02) | Whole beard at 1–2 mm | Gaps hardly show |
| Goatee (B06) | Chin | Cheeks are shaved |
| Van Dyke (B07) | Chin and moustache | Cheeks are shaved |
| Circle Beard (B08) | Around the mouth | Cheeks are shaved |
| Anchor Beard (B12) | Chin, jaw and a thin moustache | Cheeks are shaved |

### How long should I grow it before deciding?

About 4–6 weeks. Short growth always looks patchier than it really is.

**From the chair:** Don't grow a full beard to hide patches. Shape the beard around them instead.

**FAQ**

- **Does shaving make a beard grow thicker?** No. Shaving doesn't change how hair grows.
- **Which beard looks best with patchy cheeks?** A Van Dyke or a Goatee.

## P10 How to cover grey hair: jet black, natural black or grey blending?

- **URL:** /learn/cover-grey-hair · **Read:** 2 min · **Topic:** Colour
- **Meta description:** Compare jet black, natural black, dark brown and grey blending for grey hair: how it looks, time at the shop, root touch-ups and the safety test first.

**Short answer:** For full coverage that still looks natural, Natural Black (Level 2) is the safest first choice. Jet Black (Level 1) can look flat and harsh, especially on light skin. If you only want to look fresher, Grey Blending softens grey in 15–20 minutes with no hard root line. Test any permanent dye 48 hours before.

### Which option suits me?

| Option | Covers grey | Looks | At the shop | Roots show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jet Black (C01) | Fully | Strong, can look flat | 45 min | In 3–4 weeks |
| Natural Black (C02) | Fully | Natural dark hair | 45 min | In 3–4 weeks |
| Dark Brown (C03) | Fully | Softer and warmer | 45 min | In 4 weeks |
| Grey Blending (C08) | Partly, on purpose | Natural salt and pepper | 15–20 min | Fades slowly, no hard line |

### Is hair dye safe?

Most reactions are mild, and serious ones are rare. Many permanent dyes contain PPD, a common cause of reactions. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions) advises testing the dye on a strand of hair at least 48 hours before, wearing gloves, and never leaving dye on longer than the packet says. Symptoms can take up to 72 hours to appear.

### Can I colour my beard too?

Yes. Turn on "Also colour beard and moustache" in My Look. The barber keeps dye away from your lips.

**From the chair:** Going from very grey to jet black in one visit is a big jump. Start with Natural Black. You can go darker next time.

**FAQ**

- **How often do I need to touch up the roots?** Every 3–4 weeks for permanent colour.
- **I've had a black henna tattoo. Can I dye my hair?** Take extra care. Black henna paste contains high levels of PPD, which raises the risk of a reaction to hair dye, the NHS says. Do a strand test first, or choose a non-permanent dye.

## P11 Henna or hair dye: which is better for grey hair?

- **URL:** /learn/henna-vs-hair-dye · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Colour
- **Meta description:** Henna (mehndi) is a plant colour that turns grey hair red-orange. Hair dye gives black or brown. Compare results, time at the shop and safety.

**Short answer:** Choose henna if you want a plant-based colour and like a red-orange tone on grey hair. Choose hair dye if you want black or brown. Henna takes 1–2 hours and is hard to change later. Dye takes about 45 minutes and needs a strand test 48 hours before. Never use "black henna".

### How do they compare?

|  | Henna (C12) | Hair dye (C01–C04) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colour on grey | Red-orange | Black or brown |
| Made from | The henna plant | Chemicals, often including PPD |
| At the shop | 1–2 hours | About 45 min |
| Changing colour later | Hard | Easier |
| Safety check | Ask about past reactions | Strand test 48 hours before |

### Why avoid "black henna"?

Black henna is not real henna. Its paste contains high levels of PPD, which can cause skin reactions and make a later reaction to hair dye more likely, according to the [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions). Use pure henna only.

### Can I use hair dye after henna?

The result can be patchy or an unexpected colour. Tell your barber you have used henna before any dye.

**From the chair:** Want a darker result than red-orange? Ask your barber what they mix with henna and what colour it gives on grey.

**FAQ**

- **Which is the risky one?** "Black henna", because of the PPD in it. Pure henna is a plant product.
- **How long does henna last?** It doesn't wash out. Roots show in 3–4 weeks.

**Related:** C12 Henna (Mehndi) · C02 Natural Black · C08 Grey Blending

## P12 Wax, gel, clay or pomade: which hair product should you use?

- **URL:** /learn/hair-products-guide · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Care
- **Meta description:** Wax for everyday hold, gel for spikes, clay for a matte look, pomade for shine. Pick the right hair product for your style in one minute.

**Short answer:** Match the product to the finish you want. Wax gives everyday hold with a little shine. Gel holds hard and looks wet, which suits spikes. Dry wax (clay) gives a natural matte look and makes thin hair look fuller. Shiny wax (pomade) gives a smooth, polished look for slick backs and side parts.

### Which product for which style?

| Product | Hold | Finish | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wax | Medium | Light shine | Fades, Brush Up, Undercut |
| Gel | Strong | Wet look | Spiky, Mohawk |
| Dry wax (clay) | Medium to strong | Matte | French Crop, Crop Fade, thin hair |
| Shiny wax (pomade) | Medium | Shiny | Slick Back, Classic Side Part, Pompadour |
| Cream | Light | Natural | Taper Cut, Middle Part |
| Curl cream | Light | Soft curls | Curly Top Fade, Natural Curls |

### How much should I use?

Start with a pea-sized amount. Rub it between your palms until warm, then work it through from the back to the front.

### Should I wash it out every day?

Yes. Product left on the scalp builds up and can make it itchy.

**From the chair:** Most men use too much. Use less product and a little more blow drying for a better shape.

**FAQ**

- **What's best for thin hair?** Dry wax (clay). It adds grip without weighing hair down.
- **What's the difference between pomade and wax?** Pomade gives more shine. Wax is drier and easier to restyle with your fingers.

**Related:** H05 French Crop · H13 Slick Back · H16 Spiky · H26 Curly Top Fade

## P13 Which hairstyles survive a motorbike helmet?

- **URL:** /learn/helmet-hair · **Read:** 1 min · **Topic:** Care
- **Meta description:** Ride a scooter or motorbike? Short cuts with a rough top recover best after a helmet. See the styles that work and a 30-second fix.

**Short answer:** Short cuts with a rough, textured top survive a helmet best, because they bounce back with your fingers. Good choices are the Crop Fade, French Crop, Buzz Cut, Crew Cut and Curly Top Fade. Tall styles like the Pompadour and Quiff flatten fast. Keep a little dry wax with you for a 30-second fix.

### Which styles work best?

- **Crop Fade (H31) and French Crop (H05):** the messy look is the style
- **Buzz Cut (H01) and Crew Cut (H02):** nothing to flatten
- **Curly Top Fade (H26):** curls spring back

### Which styles struggle?

The Pompadour (H15), Quiff (H14) and Slick Back (H13) lose their height and shape under a helmet.

### How do I fix helmet hair fast?

1. Wet your fingertips lightly.
2. Rub a little dry wax (clay) between your palms.
3. Push the hair up and forward with your fingers.

**From the chair:** A thin cotton cap under the helmet keeps sweat and dust off your hair.

**FAQ**

- **Which product works best after a helmet?** Dry wax (clay). It restyles easily with your fingers.
- **How short should I go?** Machine #2 or #3 on the sides with 2 fingers on top recovers fastest.

**Related:** H31 Crop Fade · H05 French Crop · H01 Buzz Cut · H26 Curly Top Fade
