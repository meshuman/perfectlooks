- **Part of style:** the Moustache slot is locked.

## B01 Clean Shave

No hair on the face. Fresh and clean.

**Description:** A smooth face with no beard. Fresh and clean.

- **Pick this if:** you want the cleanest, youngest look for any job.
- **Skip if:** your skin gets red or bumpy after shaving.
- **Daily:** 5 min: shave, every day or every second day.
- **Weekly:** Nothing extra.
- **Next barber visit:** any time, for a hot-towel shave
- **Suits:** All face shapes · Any beard growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How do I avoid razor bumps?** Shave with the hair growth, use a new blade and shaving cream, then rinse with cold water.
- **How often should I shave?** Every day or every second day for a smooth face.
- **Is a barber shave better than shaving at home?** A hot-towel barber shave is closer and gentler. It's a good choice before an event.
- **Should the barber use a new blade?** Yes, always. It's fine to ask.

**Barber steps**

1. Ask: sensitive skin?
2. Use a new blade.
3. Trim long hair first with trimmer.
4. Warm towel or warm water.
5. Shaving cream. Shave with hair growth.
6. Cold water, then aftershave.

## B02 Light Stubble

Very short, like 2–3 days of growth.

**Description:** A very short beard, like 2–3 days of growth. Adds shape to the jaw but still looks clean.

- **Pick this if:** you want a little edge with almost no work. Works even with patchy growth.
- **Skip if:** your job needs a clean shave.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Trim at home twice, trimmer at 1–2 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks, to clean the lines
- **Suits:** All face shapes · Any beard growth
- **Also called:** 3-day beard
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What trimmer length is light stubble?** 1–2 mm.
- **Does light stubble work with a patchy beard?** Yes. At this length, gaps hardly show.
- **How often should I trim it?** Twice a week, to keep it at the same length.
- **Does stubble need a neck line?** Yes. A clean neck line keeps even short stubble neat.

**Barber steps**

1. Whole beard: trimmer 1–2 mm.
2. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.
3. Shave below neck line.
4. Cheeks: remove stray hair only.

## B03 Heavy Stubble

Short and even, like one week of growth.

**Description:** A short, even beard, like about one week of growth. Stronger than light stubble.

- **Pick this if:** you want a grown-up look without the care of a full beard.
- **Skip if:** your beard grows patchy.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Trim at home once, trimmer at 5 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks
- **Suits:** All face shapes · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What trimmer length is heavy stubble?** About 5 mm.
- **What is the difference between light and heavy stubble?** Light stubble is 1–2 mm, like 2–3 days of growth. Heavy stubble is about 5 mm, like a week.
- **Will heavy stubble itch?** It can in the first few days of growth. Beard oil helps.
- **Is heavy stubble OK for office?** Yes, if the cheek and neck lines are kept clean.

**Barber steps**

1. Whole beard: trimmer 5 mm.
2. Cheek line: follow natural line. Clean.
3. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.
4. Shave below neck line.

## B04 Short Boxed Beard

Short, neat beard with sharp cheek and neck lines.

**Description:** A short, neat beard with sharp lines on the cheeks and neck. Well kept and professional.

- **Pick this if:** you want a beard that looks right in the office, or more shape for a round face.
- **Skip if:** your beard grows patchy.
- **Daily:** 1 min: a few drops of beard oil.
- **Weekly:** Trim at home once: 6 mm on the sides, 9 mm on the chin.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2 weeks, to sharpen the lines
- **Suits:** Oval, round, long and heart face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What length is a short boxed beard?** 6 mm on the sides and 9 mm on the chin.
- **Why is it called a boxed beard?** The sharp cheek and neck lines give it a square, boxed shape.
- **Is a boxed beard good for a round face?** Yes. The square corners add shape.
- **How often should I trim it?** Once a week at home. See the barber every 2 weeks to sharpen the lines.

**Barber steps**

1. Sides: trimmer 6 mm.
2. Chin: trimmer 9 mm.
3. Cheek line: straight and sharp. Razor.
4. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.
5. Corners at jaw: square, not round.
6. Shave below neck line.

## B05 Full Beard

Thick, fuller beard, shaped and neat.

**Description:** A thick, full beard, shaped round or square at the bottom. Strong and mature.

- **Pick this if:** your beard grows thick and you want a strong look.
- **Skip if:** your growth is patchy or medium, or you have a long face.
- **Daily:** 3 min: beard oil and comb.
- **Weekly:** Wash with beard wash 2–3 times.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks, for shaping
- **Suits:** Oval, square and heart face · Full growth only
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How long does it take to grow a full beard?** Usually 2–3 months, depending on how fast your beard grows.
- **Do I need beard oil?** Yes. It keeps the hair soft and stops the skin underneath getting dry and itchy.
- **Round or square bottom: which is better?** A round bottom softens a square face. A square bottom suits oval and heart faces. Ask your barber.
- **How do I stop a full beard looking messy?** Comb it every day and get it shaped every 3–4 weeks.

**Barber steps**

1. Comb beard down.
2. Sides: trimmer 12 mm or scissors.
3. Chin: 2 fingers long. Longest part.
4. Ask: round or square bottom?
5. Cheek line: natural. Remove stray hair.
6. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.

## B06 Goatee

Hair on the chin only. Cheeks shaved.

**Description:** Hair only on the chin, with the cheeks shaved.

- **Pick this if:** your cheeks don't grow much hair, or you want a round face to look longer.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Shave the cheeks 2–3 times. Trim the chin at 6 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round, square and heart face · Any growth, even patchy
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is the difference between a goatee and a Van Dyke?** A goatee is chin hair only. A Van Dyke adds a separate moustache and a pointed chin.
- **Can I keep my moustache with a goatee?** Yes. Pick a style in the Moustache slot, or choose a Van Dyke or Circle Beard.
- **Is a goatee good for a patchy beard?** Yes. It only needs hair on the chin.
- **Does a goatee suit a round face?** Yes. It makes the face look longer.

**Barber steps**

1. Shave cheeks and sides clean.
2. Chin: keep width of the mouth.
3. Chin hair: trimmer 6 mm.
4. Sharp edges with trimmer.
5. Shave under the jaw.

## B07 Van Dyke

Pointed chin beard with a separate moustache. Cheeks shaved.

**Description:** A pointed chin beard with a separate moustache and shaved cheeks. Classic, with character.

- **Pick this if:** your cheeks grow patchy but your chin and lip grow well.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face.
- **Daily:** 1 min: comb the moustache, a little moustache wax if you like.
- **Weekly:** Shave the cheeks and the gap 2–3 times.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round, square and heart face · Any growth, even patchy
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How is a Van Dyke different from a circle beard?** A Van Dyke has a gap between the moustache and chin beard. A circle beard joins them.
- **How long should the chin part be?** 1–2 fingers, shaped to a point.
- **Do I need moustache wax?** It's not required, but a little wax helps the moustache keep its shape.
- **Is a Van Dyke good for a patchy beard?** Yes. It only uses the chin and upper lip.

**Barber steps**

1. Shave cheeks and sides clean.
2. Chin: 1–2 fingers long, pointed.
3. Moustache: trim above lip line.
4. Shave the gap between moustache and chin.
5. Sharp edges with trimmer.

## B08 Circle Beard

Moustache and chin beard joined in a circle. Cheeks shaved.

**Description:** Moustache and chin beard joined in a circle around the mouth. Cheeks shaved.

- **Pick this if:** you want a neat, grown-up look but your cheeks grow thin.
- **Skip if:** you have a very long face.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Shave the cheeks 2–3 times. Trim at 5 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round and square face · Any growth, even patchy
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a circle beard?** A moustache joined to a chin beard in a round shape around the mouth.
- **Is a circle beard the same as a goatee?** No. A goatee is chin hair only. A circle beard includes the moustache.
- **Is a circle beard OK for office?** Yes. It looks neat and professional.
- **How do I keep the shape round?** Shave the cheeks 2–3 times a week, and get the outline sharpened every 3 weeks.

**Barber steps**

1. Shave cheeks clean.
2. Keep moustache and chin joined.
3. Shape: round, around the mouth.
4. All hair: trimmer 5 mm.
5. Sharp outline with trimmer.
6. Shave under the jaw.

## B09 Chin Strap

Thin line of beard along the jaw, ear to ear.

**Description:** A thin line of beard along the jaw, ear to ear. Sharp and defined.

- **Pick this if:** you want a sharp jaw line.
- **Skip if:** your beard grows patchy, or you can't shave often.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Shave above and below the line 3 times.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval and round face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Side

**FAQ**

- **How wide should a chin strap be?** About 1 finger wide.
- **Does a chin strap include a moustache?** No, but you can add one in the Moustache slot.
- **Is a chin strap hard to keep?** Yes. The lines need shaving about 3 times a week.
- **Does a chin strap suit a round face?** Yes. It gives the jaw a sharper outline.

**Barber steps**

1. Line width: 1 finger.
2. Follow the jaw bone, ear to ear.
3. Line hair: trimmer 3 mm.
4. Shave cheeks above the line.
5. Shave neck below the line.

## B10 Faded Beard

Short at the top, fuller down the jaw. Goes well with a fade haircut.

**Description:** The beard is short near the ears and fuller down the jaw. It blends into a fade haircut.

- **Pick this if:** you have a fade haircut and want hair and beard to match.
- **Skip if:** your beard grows patchy.
- **Daily:** 1 min: beard oil.
- **Weekly:** Trim at home once: 6 mm on the chin and jaw.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks, with your haircut
- **Suits:** Oval, square, round and heart face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Side

**FAQ**

- **What is a faded beard?** A beard that gets shorter near the ears and blends into a fade haircut.
- **Do I need a fade haircut too?** It looks best with one, so hair and beard flow together.
- **How short is it at the top?** About 1 mm at the sideburns, blending to 6 mm along the jaw.
- **How often should I get it re-cut?** Every 2–3 weeks, together with your haircut.

**Barber steps**

1. Chin and jaw: trimmer 6 mm.
2. Sideburns at top: trimmer 1 mm.
3. Blend down: 1, 3, then 6 mm.
4. Match the hair fade at the sideburns.
5. Cheek line: clean.
6. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.

## B11 Balbo

Chin beard along the jaw with a separate moustache. No sideburns.

**Description:** A short beard on the chin and lower jaw, with a separate moustache and no sideburns.

- **Pick this if:** you want a sharp, stylish beard that shapes a round or wide face.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face, or your chin grows patchy.
- **Daily:** 1 min: beard oil, comb the moustache.
- **Weekly:** Shave the sideburns and gaps twice. Trim at 6 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round, square and heart face · Medium or full growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a Balbo beard?** A beard on the chin and lower jaw, with a separate moustache and no sideburns.
- **How is a Balbo different from a Van Dyke?** A Balbo spreads wider along the jaw. A Van Dyke stays pointed at the chin.
- **Does a Balbo suit a round face?** Yes. It adds shape to a round or wide face.
- **Is a Balbo hard to keep?** Medium effort: shave the sideburns and gaps twice a week.

**Barber steps**

1. Shave sideburns and upper cheeks.
2. Keep beard on chin and lower jaw.
3. Beard: trimmer 6 mm.
4. Moustache: trim above lip. Keep it separate.
5. Shave the gap at the mouth corners.
6. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.

## B12 Anchor Beard

Pointed chin beard with a thin moustache, shaped like an anchor.

**Description:** A pointed chin beard that runs a little along the jaw, with a thin moustache. Shaped like an anchor.

- **Pick this if:** you want a sharp style that works with patchy cheeks.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face, or no time to shave often.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Shave the cheeks 3 times. Trim the moustache at 3 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round, square and heart face · Any growth, even patchy
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **Why is it called an anchor beard?** Its pointed chin and jaw line look like a ship's anchor.
- **How is it different from a Balbo?** An anchor beard has a pointed chin and a thin moustache. A Balbo is wider along the jaw.
- **Does an anchor beard work with a patchy beard?** Yes. It only uses the chin, jaw and lip.
- **How often do I need to shave around it?** About 3 times a week.

**Barber steps**

1. Shave cheeks and sideburns clean.
2. Chin: pointed, 1 finger long.
3. Extend chin beard a little along the jaw.
4. Moustache: thin, trimmer 3 mm.
5. Keep moustache separate from chin.
6. Sharp edges with trimmer.

## B13 Ducktail

Full beard shaped to a point at the chin.

**Description:** A full beard with short sides and a longer, pointed chin.

- **Pick this if:** you have thick growth and want a full beard with a different shape.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face or patchy growth.
- **Daily:** 3 min: beard oil, comb into the point.
- **Weekly:** Wash with beard wash 2–3 times.
- **Next barber visit:** in 2–3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round, square and heart face · Full growth only
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a ducktail beard?** A full beard with short sides and a longer chin shaped to a point.
- **How long does it take to grow?** Usually about 3 months of full growth before it can be shaped.
- **Does a ducktail suit a round face?** Yes. The point makes the face look longer.
- **How do I keep the point?** Comb it down and into a point every day with a little beard oil.

**Barber steps**

1. Comb beard down.
2. Sides: trimmer 9 mm.
3. Chin: 2–3 fingers long.
4. Shape the chin to a point.
5. Cheek line: clean and natural.
6. Neck line: 2 fingers above Adam's apple.

## B14 Long Beard

Long, full beard, trimmed to keep a clean shape.

**Description:** A long, natural beard grown well past the chin, with clean edges.

- **Pick this if:** you have thick growth and the patience to grow it long.
- **Skip if:** you have a long face or patchy growth.
- **Daily:** 5 min: beard oil or balm, then comb.
- **Weekly:** Wash with beard wash 2–3 times.
- **Next barber visit:** in 4 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval, round, square and heart face · Full growth only
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **How long does it take to grow a long beard?** 6 months or more, depending on how fast your beard grows.
- **Do I still need a barber while it grows?** Yes. Shaping every 4 weeks keeps it looking cared for.
- **How do I stop a long beard tangling?** Comb it every day with beard oil or balm.
- **Can I keep a long beard at work?** Many jobs allow it if it's clean and shaped.

**Barber steps**

1. Ask: how much length to keep?
2. Comb beard down. Trim ends only.
3. Sides: shorter than the chin. Scissors.
4. Ask: round or square bottom?
5. Cheek line and neck line: clean.
6. Moustache: trim off the lip.

## B15 Soul Patch

Small patch of hair just under the lower lip.

**Description:** A small patch of hair just under the lower lip. The rest of the face is shaved.

- **Pick this if:** you want a small, creative detail. Easy even with patchy growth.
- **Skip if:** your office has strict rules.
- **Daily:** 3 min: shave around the patch, every day or every second day.
- **Weekly:** Trim the patch at 3 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3–4 weeks
- **Suits:** All face shapes · Any growth
- **Main photo:** Front

**FAQ**

- **What is a soul patch?** A small patch of hair just below the lower lip.
- **Can I add a moustache to a soul patch?** Yes. Pick any style in the Moustache slot.
- **Is a soul patch good for a patchy beard?** Yes. It only needs a little growth under the lip.
- **How big should the patch be?** About 1 finger wide.

**Barber steps**

1. Shave the whole beard area clean.
2. Keep a small patch under the lower lip.
3. Patch width: about 1 finger.
4. Patch: trimmer 3 mm.
5. Sharp edges with trimmer.

## B16 Mutton Chops

Thick sideburns down to the jaw, with the chin shaved.

**Description:** Thick sideburns down to the jaw, with the chin shaved. A bold, old-style look.

- **Pick this if:** you have a long face and want it to look wider.
- **Skip if:** you have a round face or patchy cheek growth.
- **Daily:** Nothing.
- **Weekly:** Shave the chin 2–3 times. Trim the sideburns at 6 mm.
- **Next barber visit:** in 3 weeks
- **Suits:** Oval and long face · Full growth only
- **Main photo:** Side

**FAQ**

- **What are mutton chops?** Thick sideburns that grow down to the jaw, with the chin shaved.
- **Do mutton chops include a moustache?** No. You can add one in the Moustache slot.
- **Do mutton chops suit a round face?** No. They make the face look wider.
- **How do I keep them neat?** Trim them at 6 mm every week and shave the chin 2–3 times a week.

**Barber steps**

1. Keep sideburns full, down to the jaw.
2. Width at the jaw: 2–3 fingers.
3. Sideburns: trimmer 6 mm.
4. Shave chin and front of jaw clean.
5. Shave the neck.
6. Sharp edges with razor.
