## How do I check my face shape?

1. Push your hair off your forehead.
2. Find the widest part: forehead, cheekbones or jaw.
3. Compare the length of your face with its width.
4. Match what you see to the table.

| Face shape | How to tell | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oval | A bit longer than wide, soft jaw | Almost any style |
| Round | Length and width about the same, soft jaw | Height on top: Quiff, Faux Hawk, Brush Up |
| Square | Strong, wide jaw | Short, neat cuts: Crew Cut, Classic Side Part |
| Long | Much longer than wide | A fringe or fuller sides: French Crop, Caesar Cut, Two-Block |
| Heart | Wide forehead, narrow chin | A fringe, and more beard at the chin: Side Fringe, Full Beard |
