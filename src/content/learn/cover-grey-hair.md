## Is hair dye safe?

Most reactions are mild, and serious ones are rare. Many permanent dyes contain PPD, a common cause of reactions. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions) advises testing the dye on a strand of hair at least 48 hours before, wearing gloves, and never leaving dye on longer than the packet says. Symptoms can take up to 72 hours to appear.
