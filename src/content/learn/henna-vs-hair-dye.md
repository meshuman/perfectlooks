## How do they compare?

|  | Henna (C12) | Hair dye (C01–C04) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colour on grey | Red-orange | Black or brown |
| Made from | The henna plant | Chemicals, often including PPD |
| At the shop | 1–2 hours | About 45 min |
| Changing colour later | Hard | Easier |
| Safety check | Ask about past reactions | Strand test 48 hours before |

## Why avoid "black henna"?

Black henna is not real henna. Its paste contains high levels of PPD, which can cause skin reactions and make a later reaction to hair dye more likely, according to the [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions). Use pure henna only.

## Can I use hair dye after henna?

The result can be patchy or an unexpected colour. Tell your barber you have used henna before any dye.
