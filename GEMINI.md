# Image Generation Guidelines & Preferences

When generating images for the hairstyle catalogue (Arjun character reference and all style items):

1. **Skin Tone**:
   - The character (Arjun) must have a **fairer skin tone** (fair/light-medium with natural warm undertones, rather than medium-brown or dark).
   - Maintain natural skin texture and realism without over-smoothing or plastic appearance.

2. **Lighting**:
   - Use **professional studio lighting**: bright, well-balanced commercial studio setup.
   - Diffused bright key light, clean soft fill to eliminate harsh dark shadows, and a subtle rim/edge light to cleanly separate hair and shoulders from the backdrop.
