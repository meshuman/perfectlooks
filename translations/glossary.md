## Names

- **Style names** (hair, beard, moustache, colour) stay in English: customers and barbers use them, and the names match across languages.
- **Ready Look names** are translated, because they describe the look (Office Ready = अफिसका लागि तयार).

## Tone

- Customers: polite form (तपाईं, -नुहोस्).
- Barber steps: short commands, the same polite form, 6 words or fewer where possible.
